Terms of Service

ClipCityVice Shorts Publisher is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single individual creator to publish their own short-form video content, created from their own footage and commentary, to their own authorized social media accounts (including TikTok).

  • The tool runs locally on the operator's own computer. It is not a public service, has no public signup, and is not distributed to or used by any third party.
  • It only posts content that the operator has created and selected, to accounts the operator has authorized through TikTok's own OAuth login flow.
  • By authorizing this app via TikTok, the operator agrees to be bound by TikTok's own Developer Terms of Service, which govern use of the TikTok API.
  • The operator may revoke this app's access to their TikTok account at any time from their TikTok account settings.

Privacy Policy

This tool does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data belonging to anyone other than its operator, and does not serve any user other than its operator.

  • What is stored: an OAuth access token and refresh token issued by TikTok for the operator's own account, saved locally on the operator's own computer in a configuration file. These are used only to authorize video uploads to that same account.
  • What is not collected: no analytics, no third-party data sharing, no advertising identifiers, and no data about any TikTok viewer, follower, or other user.
  • Data retention: the stored tokens remain only on the operator's local machine and are deleted if the operator removes the local configuration file or revokes access via TikTok.
